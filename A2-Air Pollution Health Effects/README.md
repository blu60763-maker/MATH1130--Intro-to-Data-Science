# Air Pollution & Global Health Impact Analysis

## Introduction

### Business Context
Air pollution is a critical global issue contributing directly to global warming and severe public health crises. Despite multinational initiatives to improve air quality, progress remains slow—largely driven by the continued combustion of fossil fuels such as coal, alongside economic and political hurdles that stall renewable energy adoption.

As a **Data Science Consultant** for a prominent environmental organization, this project focuses on analyzing the **plausible impacts of air contamination on the health of the global population**. Using raw datasets provided by:
* [World Health Organization (WHO)](https://www.who.int/)
* [Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME)](https://www.healthdata.org/)
* [World Bank Group](https://www.worldbank.org/)

The primary objective is to extract actionable insights and produce clear data visualizations to support public awareness campaigns and grant applications.

---

### Analytical Context
The project processes a raw data repository (`files/`) characterized by high dimensionality, disorganization, and fragmented documentation across several datasets.

#### Core Objectives
1. **Data Cleaning & Wrangling:** Extract, combine, and clean heterogeneous datasets into structured analytical formats.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Formulate data-driven hypotheses, generate informative statistical plots, and assess correlations among key pollution indicators and public health metrics.

---

## Research Questions

This study specifically investigates the following core questions:

1. **Global Trends:** Are we making measurable progress in reducing emitted pollutants worldwide?
2. **Geographic Targeting:** Which critical regions exhibit the highest contamination levels and should be prioritized for environmental campaigns?
3. **Health Outcomes:** Is there observable progress in preventing air pollution-related mortality over time?
4. **Demographic Factors:** Which demographic and socioeconomic characteristics correlate most strongly with health issues derived from air pollution?
